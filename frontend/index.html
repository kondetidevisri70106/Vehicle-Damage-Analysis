Frequently Asked Questions

What types of vehicle damage can the system detect?

The system is designed to detect common visible vehicle damage such as dents, scratches, cracks, bumper damage, glass damage, and other component-level damage depending on the trained model and available views.

Why should I upload multiple vehicle views?

Multiple views provide broader visual coverage and help the system combine evidence from different perspectives. Damage visible in one view might be partially obscured in another.

What is hidden-damage risk?

Hidden-damage risk is an estimate of the possibility of damage that may not be directly visible from the uploaded images, especially when impact sensor information indicates significant force.

How does IoT data improve the analysis?

Impact and motion-related sensor information provides additional accident context. Combined with visible damage, it helps the system estimate severity and hidden damage risk more reliably.

How is repair cost estimated?

The displayed cost is a system-generated approximation based on detected damage, component, severity, and repair action. It does not replace a professional quotation.

Can the system generate an insurance report?

Yes. The system organizes detection results, severity, hidden-damage risk, images, sensor data, and repair estimates into a structured inspection report.

Can the system guarantee that no hidden damage exists?

No. The system provides a risk estimate and does not replace professional mechanical inspection. Always have significant damage confirmed by a qualified technician.
